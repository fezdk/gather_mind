Gather Mind

Support

Gather Mind 0.6.0 is an Android-first beta. It works offline and does not require an account or backend.

Appearance

Gather Mind follows the phone's light or dark appearance by default. Choose a fixed mode under Settings & privacy → Appearance if preferred; the choice is stored only on the phone.

Home-screen widget

Long-press an empty area of the Android home screen and choose Widgets → Gather Mind. Resize it to switch between the compact completed/total count and the larger contextual layout. If it has not refreshed yet, open Gather Mind once. Titles are hidden by default; enable them under Settings & privacy → Home screen only when they may safely be visible without unlocking the app.

Reminder troubleshooting

  1. Confirm Android notifications are enabled for Gather Mind.
  2. On Android 14 and later, check Settings → Apps → Special app access → Alarms & reminders. Without that access, Android may deliver a reminder a little later than requested.
  3. Check Focus, Do Not Disturb, and battery-saving settings.
  4. If needed, exclude Gather Mind from the phone manufacturer's aggressive battery optimisation.
  5. Open and save an appointment again after changing permission settings.

Quiet daily status is optional and off by default. Enable it and choose its time under Settings & privacy → Daily goals. It appears without sound or vibration only when goals remain unfinished, and it never shows their titles.

Data and deletion

All content is held in an encrypted database local to the phone, and the widget's bounded summary is encrypted separately. There is no server copy or recovery password. Delete all local data permanently removes all content, the widget summary, and scheduled Gather Mind reminders.

App lock troubleshooting

  • Lock Gather Mind is optional and is enabled in Settings & privacy after a successful strong fingerprint or secure face check.
  • When a locked app opens or returns after its timeout, the biometric prompt starts automatically. Cancelling it leaves the app locked; use Unlock Gather Mind when ready to try again.
  • If biometric authentication is temporarily locked, unlock the phone normally and try again.
  • If every enrolled fingerprint or face was removed, add one again in the phone's settings before opening Gather Mind. Changing biometric enrollment does not delete the encrypted database.
  • Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the only local copy. Android cloud backup is disabled.

For support, open an issue in the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not include private app content or device backups in a public issue.
