Gather Mind

Privacy policy

Effective 23 August 2026 · Gather Mind 0.6.0

Your data stays encrypted on your device. Gather Mind does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Gather Mind is a local-first organisational app maintained by Nezar. It works without an account or backend and contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDK, cloud sync, or account system. The Android release does not request Internet access.

Information stored on the device

Thoughts, tags, goals, appointments, locations, and appointment-plan items are stored in an encrypted database in the app's private storage on the user's device. A random database key is held in the operating system's secure key store. Reminder details and an optional generic count of unfinished goals are given to the device operating system solely so it can deliver notifications requested by the user. The quiet status never contains goal titles.

On Android, the home-screen widget uses a bounded local summary rather than opening the main database. That summary is encrypted separately with Android Keystore. Its default form contains counts and appointment times but no goal or appointment titles. Users can explicitly allow titles, with a warning that home-screen content is visible without Gather Mind's biometric lock.

Permissions

  • Notification permission is used only for locally scheduled appointment reminders and the optional quiet daily goal status.
  • Exact-alarm access lets Android deliver chosen local notification times accurately; Android falls back to less exact timing if that access is unavailable.
  • Biometric authentication is used only on the device when the user chooses to enable Lock Gather Mind. Gather Mind does not receive or store fingerprint or face data.

The rest of the app remains usable if notification permission is denied, and the optional biometric app lock is off by default.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user deletes individual items, selects Settings & privacy → Delete all local data, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. The in-app deletion control also removes the encrypted widget summary, cancels Gather Mind's scheduled reminders, and dismisses its delivered notifications. Android operating-system backup is disabled for the app.

Children, health, and sensitive content

Gather Mind is a general organisational aid. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. Users decide what they enter; no entered content is sent to the developer.

Security

The local database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Its random 256-bit key is stored separately in the operating system's secure key store and is not bound to the user's biometric profile. Users can optionally require strong fingerprint or secure face authentication whenever Gather Mind returns from the background. No local-only design can protect data on a compromised device or while an authorised user has the app open.

Open-source licence

Contact
