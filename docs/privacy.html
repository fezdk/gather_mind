Gather Mind

Privacy policy

Effective 18 August 2026 · Gather Mind 0.5.1

Your data stays on your device. Gather Mind does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Gather Mind is a local-first organisational app maintained by Nezar. It works without an account or backend and contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDK, cloud sync, or account system. The Android release does not request Internet access.

Information stored on the device

Thoughts, tags, goals, appointments, locations, and appointment-plan items are stored only in the app's private storage on the user's device. Reminder details are given to the device operating system solely so it can deliver reminders requested by the user.

Permissions

  • Notification permission is used only for locally scheduled reminders.
  • Exact-alarm access is used on Android only to deliver chosen appointment reminders at the requested time.

The rest of the app remains usable if notification permission is denied.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user deletes individual items, selects Settings & privacy → Delete all local data, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. The in-app deletion control also cancels Gather Mind's scheduled reminders and dismisses its delivered notifications. Android operating-system backup is disabled for the app.

Children, health, and sensitive content

Gather Mind is a general organisational aid. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. Users decide what they enter; no entered content is sent to the developer.

Security

The app relies on the device's application sandbox and operating-system security. Anyone who can unlock the device may be able to open the app, so users should enable a device passcode or biometric lock when storing sensitive notes.

Open-source licence

Gather Mind's source code is available under the Apache License 2.0. That software licence does not give the developer, contributors, or recipients any rights to a user's personal content.

Contact

Privacy and support questions can be sent to https://github.com/fezdk/gather_mind/issues.

If optional sync, accounts, analytics, or any other data transfer is added, this policy and the in-app notice will be updated before that feature is released.
